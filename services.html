Engineering
Services

From conceptual BIM models to fabrication-ready documentation — we deliver complete structural steel engineering services with precision, speed, and zero-tolerance accuracy.

01
01 Structural Steel Detailing Core Service

Complete shop drawings, erection plans, and general arrangement drawings produced to AISC, AS/NZS 1554, BS 5950, and Eurocode 3 standards. Every drawing package is checked for constructability and reviewed against the engineering model before release. We cover all structural member types — columns, beams, bracing, portals, purlins, and girts — with full connection detail callouts, weld symbols, and bolt specifications.

Shop Drawings Erection Plans GA Drawings Connection Details AISC / AS / BS / EC3
02 3D BIM Modelling Technology

Precision parametric 3D structural models using Tekla Structures and Autodesk Revit. Our models are built to LOD 350–400 and support full clash detection with architectural and MEP disciplines. All models are delivered with IFC and CIS/2 exports for open BIM coordination. Quantity take-offs, material schedules, and piece mark lists are extracted directly from the model to ensure 100% accuracy across all deliverables.

Tekla Structures Autodesk Revit IFC Export Clash Detection LOD 350–400 BOQ Extraction
03 Connection Design Engineering

Rigorous engineering of moment connections, shear connections, base plates, splice plates, gusset plates, and fin plate connections. Each connection is designed from first principles using code-compliant software, with supporting engineering calculations provided. We coordinate closely with the SER to ensure all connection forces are correctly interpreted and that fabrication detailing reflects the design intent without ambiguity.

Moment Connections Base Plates Splice Design Engineering Calcs Code Compliance
04 Fabrication & NC Support Manufacturing

NC files, DSTV/NC1 data, CNC cutting data, and material lists generated directly from the certified 3D model. This eliminates manual data re-entry at the fabrication stage and significantly reduces the risk of dimensional errors. We support full shop integration with leading fabrication management systems. Piece mark schedules, paint and surface treatment schedules, and shipping lists are all produced as part of the fabrication documentation package.

NC / DSTV Files CNC Data Material Schedules Paint Schedules Shipping Lists
05 Industrial Structures Industrial

Specialised detailing for pipe racks, conveyor structures, overhead crane runway beams, equipment platforms, mezzanine floors, industrial stair towers, and heavy industrial steelwork for process and manufacturing facilities. We understand the critical tolerances and load path requirements of industrial structures and build these considerations into every detail we produce.

Pipe Racks Conveyor Structures Crane Beams Equipment Platforms Mezzanine Floors
06 Multi-Storey Buildings Commercial

Complete detailing for multi-level commercial, residential, mixed-use, and institutional steel frame structures. This includes structural floor systems (composite, stub girder, open web joists), stair and balustrade assemblies, façade framing, curtain wall support steelwork, and rooftop plant support structures. We produce erection sequences, temporary works provisions, and lifting lug details where required.

Composite Floors Stairs & Balustrades Facade Framing Erection Sequence Lifting Lugs
07 RFI & Revision Support Support

Rapid, responsive RFI management and change order processing throughout the construction phase. We maintain live models and drawing registers that are updated in real-time. Revision clouds are clearly marked, revision histories are maintained, and all affected drawings are re-issued within agreed turnaround times. Our engineers are available for direct communication with site teams and fabricators to resolve technical queries efficiently.

RFI Responses Change Orders Live Model Updates Revision Management
