Our Story

Precision as a
Standard,
not a Goal.

Fabri-Tech Engineering was founded with a single mission: to deliver structural steel detailing services of uncompromising quality. What started as a small team of passionate engineers has grown into a globally recognised practice, working on projects across multiple continents.

We operate at the intersection of engineering discipline and digital innovation. Our teams leverage the latest BIM technologies while maintaining the exacting standards that structural fabricators demand — zero-tolerance accuracy, clear documentation, and proactive communication at every stage of the project lifecycle.

From 10,000-tonne industrial complexes to intricate mezzanine floor systems, every project receives the same rigorous attention. Our clients return not because they have to, but because the standard we set is one they can rely on — project after project, year after year.

How We Work

A Process Built
for Fabricators.

Our detailing process is built from the fabricator's perspective. We know that a drawing that can't be built — or worse, one that gets built incorrectly — costs everyone. That's why every detail we produce is checked against constructability, dimensional tolerance, and erection sequence before it leaves our desk.

We embed ourselves in our clients' project programmes. We attend coordination meetings, respond to RFIs same-day, and issue revised packages with the speed that modern construction schedules demand. We don't just deliver files — we deliver certainty.

What Drives Us

Core Values

01
Precision First

Every dimension, weld symbol, and bolt specification is checked, double-checked, and verified before release. There are no acceptable errors in structural engineering.

02
Client Partnership

We integrate directly with our clients' project teams, acting as an extension of their engineering department — not a distant contractor issuing files from afar.

03
Continuous Innovation

We invest relentlessly in technology and training. Our team stays at the forefront of BIM capabilities, software updates, and evolving detailing methodology.

04
On-Time Delivery

Construction schedules don't move for late drawings. We plan our deliverables rigorously and communicate proactively whenever anything threatens a committed deadline.

05
Safety by Design

Connection sequences, lifting provisions, and erection aids are built into every package. We design details with constructability and site safety in mind from the first model.

06
Long-Term Thinking

We build relationships, not transactions. The repeat business and referrals that drive our growth are the best measure of the trust our clients place in us.
